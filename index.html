Every plan links to where its price and benefits were checked, mostly TelecomTalk's 2026 plan lists, using the newest listing wherever sources disagree. Plans marked "Check in app" have sources that disagree or are older than August 2026. Operators change plans often, so confirm the price in your operator's app before you pay. Data-only add-ons, ISD packs and feature-phone-only plans are left out. Plans sold only in some states appear only when you pick one of those states. Network rankings come from Opensignal's February 2026 India report, which names the winning operator in each of 22 regions for reliability, download speed, video, gaming and 5G; signal in your own building can differ. BSNL runs 4G only. Vi gives unlimited 5G on plans from ₹299 only where it has launched 5G.
